# Source provenance and scope

This package contains authored guidance, examples and summaries, not copies of
licensed standards. External standards and publications remain the property of
their owners and are not licensed by this repository's MIT license.

- `[V: ...]` is an original-author research annotation. It does not mean every
  claim or current edition was independently reverified for this release.
- `(summary)` identifies secondary reporting or a synthesis.
- `(verify)` identifies a claim, numeric starting point or attribution requiring
  independent checking. Some example citations originally pointed to research
  notes that are not distributed; those annotations have been downgraded.

Use the public sources at the end of each reference. If a claim lacks a usable
primary source, treat it as unverified. Identify the edition, clause and domain
before representing a numeric value as a requirement. Nuclear-control-room,
aviation, medical and military guidance does not automatically govern another
machine or jurisdiction. Do not infer conformity, a performance level, or a SIL
from these skills or their scripts.

Review the machine's risk assessment and applicable primary sources for safety
functions. These instructions do not authorize operating equipment, bypassing
interlocks, disconnecting a live HMI, or changing a running installation. Such
tests need an approved test procedure and the appropriate equipment personnel.

The entry-point workflow is intentionally brief; load only the references that
change the decision. The companion light and Leptos skills are independently
installable, so discover them through the agent's skill catalog rather than
assuming a workstation-specific filesystem location.
